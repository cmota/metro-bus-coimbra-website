Your stop, every line, the next bus at a glance.

MetroBus Coimbra puts one station’s departures on a single board, towards the centre and towards Serpins, with the Blue, Red and Green lines together, and says where each bus ends. Choose your station or let your location find the nearest. Timetables come from Metro Mondego’s official open data and, once downloaded, work with no signal.

Departures

One station, every line, both directions. Solum on a weekday morning: To Coimbra B · República and To Serpins on the same board, each departure with its run, U1 or S2, and where it ends.

Timetable

Every published minute for your station and direction, on weekdays, Saturdays and Sundays, with all the lines in one grid. The next departure is marked; past ones are greyed out.

Map

The three lines drawn on a map that ships inside the app, so it works with no signal, with each bus shown where the timetable says it should be. Tap a station to open its board.

Plan a trip

Choose any two stations and get the next journeys, direct or with one change when that gets you there sooner, each shown stop by stop with where to change, and the fare the trip needs.

Choose station

Search, the stations near you, favourites, and all 37 with their line badges. A fresh install asks you to choose; it never guesses a station for you.

Features

Built around one question: when is my next bus?

Everything else in the app exists to answer that question better.

Your station, in focus

Choose your station, or use your location to find the nearest one. The app remembers your choice, and a star keeps the stops you use most a tap away.

Every line, one board

The Blue, Red and Green lines share most of their route, so the board puts them together and groups by direction, towards the centre and towards Serpins, so you can see whichever bus comes first. Every departure shows its run, U1 or S2, in its line’s colour, and says where it ends: Coimbra B, República, Serpins, Lousã - Estação, Corvo or Vale das Flores.

The whole timetable

Every published departure for your station and direction, on weekdays, Saturdays and Sundays, with all the lines in the same grid. Tap an upcoming departure for its details; past ones are greyed out.

Reminders that count the walk

For the bus coming within the hour, set a reminder five minutes before it leaves, or one timed to your estimated walk plus two minutes’ spare, so it fires when you need to start walking.

Two maps of one network

A diagram of the lines for finding your place on the route, with the zone changes marked, and a geographic map for finding it in the city. The map ships inside the app, so both work offline, and tapping a station opens the same station page.

Fares as the board prints them

Municipal, Intermunicipal and Regional: a pre-purchased ticket costs €1, €2 or €3 and is valid for 90, 120 or 150 minutes; monthly passes are €30, €35 or €40, with day passes too. The planner names the fare your trip needs, and every station shows its municipality.

On the home screen

Widgets put the next scheduled departure on your Android or iOS home screen. On supported iPhones a Live Activity can show it on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and Siri can answer the next departure from the saved timetable.

Works with no signal

Download the timetable once and the countdown works in a tunnel or with data off. The app checks for a newer timetable when you open it and only downloads one when the operator has published it.

Only what the operator has said

A station page lists its lines, its fare municipality and its ticket machine, which Metro Mondego has at every station. Line status comes from the operator’s own notices, and when a check fails the app says so instead of showing an all-clear. A station closed for works is marked and never offered as a place to get on or off.

Estimated, not live

Estimated from the timetable, not tracked live.

There is no live feed of the Metrobus buses in this app, and it does not pretend to have one. Countdowns come from the published timetable and your phone’s clock. When the app shows where a bus should be, that is an estimate, and it says so.

What the app does

  • Reads Metro Mondego’s official timetable, published as NeTEx open data.
  • Counts down to the next departure, with no network.
  • Estimates which two stops a bus should be between, and labels it as estimated.
  • Shows the operator’s own published notices when there are any.

What the app does not do

  • It does not follow the bus by GPS. There is no real-time vehicle position.
  • It does not know about delays or cancellations the operator has not published.
  • It does not sell tickets or passes. Fares are reference only, with links to the operator.
  • It does not send your location anywhere. It stays on the phone.
The network

Three lines, thirty-seven stations.

Since 11 September 2026 the network runs as three colour-coded lines. The app knows all three, and treats them as one wherever they stop at the same place. Blue, Red and Green, as on the station signage.

Information

Made by a rider, not the operator.

MetroBus Coimbra is not an official Metro Mondego app and has no affiliation with the operator. It was built by someone who rides the Metrobus and wanted a simpler way to catch it.

Official Metro Mondego Open data (dados.gov.pt)

Timetables and stops come from the NeTEx dataset Metro Mondego publishes on the Portuguese government open-data portal, dados.gov.pt. This app is not an official source of public information: for official timetables, fares and notices, see Metro Mondego and SMTUC.

Privacy, in three lines

There is no account and no sign-up. Location is used on-device only, to find your nearest station and estimate your walk to it.

Your chosen station and favourites are saved on your phone. Departure reminders use the system’s notifications.

The app’s network calls download the public timetable and the operator’s service notices from the project’s server. The map is bundled. No advertising SDKs, no tracking libraries, no analytics.
