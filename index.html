Your stop, both lines, the next bus at a glance.

MetroBus Coimbra puts one station’s departures on a single board, To Portagem and To Serpins, Urban and Suburban together, and says where each bus ends. Choose your station or let your location find the nearest. Timetables come from Metro Mondego’s official open data and, once downloaded, work with no signal.

Features

Built around one question: when is my next bus?

Everything else in the app exists to answer that question better.

Your station, in focus

Choose your station, or use your location to find the nearest one. The app remembers your choice, and a star keeps the stops you use most a tap away.

Both lines, one board

Between Vale das Flores and Portagem, Urban and Suburban serve the same stops. The board merges them and groups by direction, To Portagem and To Serpins, so you see the bus that comes first. Each departure names where it ends: Urban buses turn back at Vale das Flores, and some Suburban buses end at Lousã - Estação or Corvo.

The whole timetable

Every published departure for your station and direction, on weekdays, Saturdays and Sundays, with both lines in the same grid. Tap an upcoming departure for its details; past ones are greyed out.

Reminders that count the walk

For the bus coming within the hour, set a reminder five minutes before it leaves, or one timed to your estimated walk plus two minutes’ spare, so it fires when you need to start walking.

Two maps of one network

A line diagram for finding your place on the route, with the zone changes marked, and a geographic map for finding it in the city. The map ships inside the app, so both work offline, and tapping a station opens the same station page.

Fares as the board prints them

Municipal, Intermunicipal and Regional: a pre-purchased ticket costs €1, €2 or €3 and is valid for 90, 120 or 150 minutes; monthly passes are €30, €35 or €40, with day passes too. The planner names the fare your trip needs, and every station shows its municipality.

On the home screen

Widgets put the next scheduled departure on your Android or iOS home screen. On supported iPhones a Live Activity can show it on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and Siri can answer the next departure from the saved timetable.

Works with no signal

Download the timetable once and the countdown works in a tunnel or with data off. The app checks for a newer timetable when you open it and only downloads one when the operator has published it.

Only what the operator has said

A station page lists its lines, its municipality, and a ticket machine or office only where Metro Mondego has announced one. Line status comes from the operator’s own notices, and when a check fails the app says so instead of showing an all-clear.

Estimated, not live

Estimated from the timetable, not tracked live.

There is no live feed of the Metrobus buses in this app, and it does not pretend to have one. Countdowns come from the published timetable and your phone’s clock. When the app shows where a bus should be, that is an estimate, and it says so.

What the app does

  • Reads Metro Mondego’s official timetable, published as NeTEx open data.
  • Counts down to the next departure, with no network.
  • Estimates which two stops a bus should be between, and labels it as estimated.
  • Shows the operator’s own published notices when there are any.

What the app does not do

  • It does not follow the bus by GPS. There is no real-time vehicle position.
  • It does not know about delays or cancellations the operator has not published.
  • It does not sell tickets or passes. Fares are reference only, with links to the operator.
  • It does not send your location anywhere. It stays on the phone.
The network

Two lines, twenty-eight stations.

The app knows both, and treats them as one wherever they stop at the same place. Yellow for Urban and blue for Suburban, as on the station signage.

The last ten stops of the Suburban line are the whole Urban line, so between Vale das Flores and Portagem both lines stop. The app’s own summary: on weekdays a bus about every 10 minutes on each line by day and every 30 in the evening, from about 06:00 until after midnight; at weekends the Urban line every 15 to 30 minutes and the Suburban every 30 to 60. Intervals vary by station, direction and hour, so check your station’s timetable. The line crosses three municipalities, Coimbra, Miranda do Corvo and Lousã, and a ticket is priced by how many it crosses.

Information

Made by a rider, not the operator.

MetroBus Coimbra is not an official Metro Mondego app and has no affiliation with the operator. It was built by someone who rides the Metrobus and wanted a simpler way to catch it.

Official Metro Mondego

Privacy, in three lines

There is no account and no sign-up. Location is used on-device only, to find your nearest station and estimate your walk to it.

Your chosen station and favourites are saved on your phone. Departure reminders use the system’s notifications.

The app’s network calls download the public timetable and the operator’s service notices from the project’s server. The map is bundled. No advertising SDKs, no tracking libraries, no analytics.
